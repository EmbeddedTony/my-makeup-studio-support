A little help in your studio

Let’s get you creating.

Need help with My Makeup Studio? Email contact@operationembedded.net. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version and what happened. Please do not send payment details or face photos.

How do I preview makeup on my own face?

Tap +, then Import a photo or Take a photo. Use a clear, well-lit, front-facing portrait. Choose a preset or makeup tool and adjust its shade and intensity. Tap Before / after to see the original. For a group photo, choose a face before editing.

The makeup does not line up with my face.

Try a photo with the full face visible, even lighting and less head tilt. Sunglasses, hair covering the eyes and very small faces can make detection harder. You can also zoom in and paint by hand. A photo preview is illustrative and is not a guarantee of how a physical cosmetic will look.

How do hair colors and Apple Pencil work?

Choose Hair and paint over the hair to make a reusable mask. Change the shade without repainting, or erase parts of the mask. Hair is not automatically segmented. On a compatible iPad, use Apple Pencil for brushwork; Pencil-only mode helps avoid finger marks. Foundation remains a sheer complexion tint.

Free tools and optional purchases

Free includes 216 shades, custom colors, basic makeup, matte/satin/gloss finishes, four eyeliner styles, hair dye colors, face selection, three saved looks and watermark-free export of free effects. All effects can be previewed for free. Saving or exporting an effect from a paid pack requires that pack or Premium.

  • Sparkle Studio: shimmer and glitter finishes — US $1.99, one time.
  • Glow Studio: metallic and glow finishes — US $1.99, one time.
  • Liner Lab: puppy, smudged, double wing and graphic eyeliner — US $1.99, one time.
  • Hair Studio: sparkly, metallic gold and metallic silver hair — US $1.99, one time.
  • Premium: every current and future pack and unlimited saved looks — US $2.99/month, US $14.99/year or US $29.99 lifetime.

Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Lifetime and individual packs are one-time purchases. Localized prices and applicable taxes appear in the app and Apple’s purchase confirmation; prices may vary by country or region. No purchase is needed to use the free tools.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel a subscription?

Open + → Packs & Premium → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. To cancel a subscription, use Apple’s subscription settings. Canceling prevents the next renewal; access continues through the paid period. Lifetime and packs do not renew. For billing or refund requests, visit Apple’s Report a Problem.

How do I remove a saved photo?

Open Saved looks and delete the look. This removes its photo, thumbnail, face landmarks and edits from the app. Exported copies and backups are separate; manage them in the relevant destination or Apple settings. See the Privacy Policy.

My Makeup Studio requires iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. Some device capabilities, including Apple Pencil and camera availability, depend on your hardware.
