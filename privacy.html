Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026. My Makeup Studio is an iPhone and iPad app developed by Anthony Peytchev. Contact: contact@operationembedded.net.

Photos and facial data

The app processes the photos you select or take and detects facial landmarks on your device to place makeup. Landmarks describe locations such as eyes, lips and cheeks. They are used for editing, not identifying a person. The app does not upload, share or sell your photos, face landmarks, hair masks or editing data to the developer or to an AI service. Your images are not used to train AI.

Storage and deletion

Opening a photo lets you edit it in the app. When you choose Save look, the app stores the original photo, a thumbnail, facial landmarks and editing settings in its local documents. These remain until you delete the saved look or remove the app. Delete a look in Saved looks to remove its associated local photo, thumbnail and editing files. There is no developer account or server copy to delete.

Your device’s backups may include app documents according to your Apple settings. Backups and copies exported to Photos or other destinations are controlled separately. Manage those copies through the relevant Apple settings or destination. Deleting a saved look does not delete an exported copy or a prior device backup.

Permissions and sharing

The system photo picker gives the app access to the images you choose. Camera access is requested only when you choose Take a photo. You can revoke camera permission in Settings. Export uses Apple’s share sheet and sends the image only to the destination you select; choosing Save Image may request permission to add to Photos. Do not share photos of others without their permission.

Purchases

Apple handles in-app purchases, billing and payment details. The app reads verified transaction entitlements, such as which packs you own and when a subscription expires, to unlock features and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple’s handling of purchase and backup data.

No tracking or analytics in the app

My Makeup Studio has no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or required user accounts. The app does not send usage or face data to the developer. Optional system diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are governed by your Apple settings.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you voluntarily include are used to respond and resolve your request. You do not need to send face photos for support. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations; email us to request deletion. This website does not include advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed into GitHub. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Practice portraits and changes

Built-in practice portraits are fictional AI-generated adults. They are bundled images, not generated from your photos. We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. Questions about privacy or deletion can be sent to contact@operationembedded.net.
